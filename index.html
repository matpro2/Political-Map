How it works

Move the three sliders to reflect your personal values. Each slider represents a tension between two opposing political tendencies. Once you click Calculate, the site finds which ideology — or blend of ideologies — is closest to your combination of values in a 3D political space.

The three axes
Competition Empathy

Do you believe society works best when individuals compete freely, or when people prioritise cooperation and collective wellbeing? High empathy favours solidarity and social safety nets; high competition favours meritocracy and individual responsibility.

Conservatism Curiosity

Do you value tradition, stability and proven institutions, or do you embrace change, experimentation and new ideas? Conservatism protects what exists; curiosity questions and reimagines it.

Pragmatism Idealism

Do you prefer realistic, step-by-step solutions within existing systems, or do you pursue a radically better world even at the cost of disruption? Pragmatists work with what is; idealists work toward what could be.
